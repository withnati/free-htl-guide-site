Exam-week approach

  • Use the official content guideline to confirm no major topic has been skipped.
  • Review concise tables and missed-question rules rather than rereading everything.
  • Practice choosing the best answer, not merely a technically possible answer.
  • Watch for questions that begin with specimen type, downstream assay, artifact, or control result.
  • Do not change well-established answers based only on anxiety; change when you identify a concrete reasoning error.
  • Protect sleep and logistics. Preparation quality falls when the final days become an all-night cram.
