Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 30, 2026

Overview

Free HTL Guide is an independent educational website that provides histology study materials, downloadable resources, and updates for HT and HTL candidates. This policy explains what information may be collected when you use the site and how that information may be handled.

Information collected

Depending on how you use the site, the following information may be collected:

  • Your email address when you request study resources, join a waitlist, or subscribe for updates.
  • Information you voluntarily include in a contact form or email.
  • Basic technical and usage information if privacy-friendly analytics are enabled in the future, such as visited pages, referral source, browser type, device type, and approximate location.

How information is used

  • To deliver requested study resources or updates.
  • To respond to questions, feedback, or support requests.
  • To improve lessons, navigation, downloads, and site performance.
  • To protect the site from misuse, spam, or technical abuse.

Forms and third-party services

The site currently uses Formspree to process certain form submissions. Information submitted through a form may therefore be processed by Formspree according to its own privacy and security practices. Downloadable files are hosted through GitHub Pages.

Analytics

The current site does not intentionally run active visitor analytics by default. If analytics are enabled later, this policy will be updated to identify the provider and describe the information collected. Browser-based “Do Not Track” signals may be respected where technically supported.

Cookies and local storage

The site may use your browser’s local storage to remember preferences such as light or dark mode. This preference stays in your browser unless you clear it. The site does not currently use advertising cookies.

Data sharing

Personal information is not sold. Information may be shared only with service providers needed to operate the website or deliver requested resources, or when disclosure is required by law.

Data retention

Information is kept only as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, to maintain records, or to meet legal and operational obligations.

Your choices

  • You may choose not to submit optional forms.
  • You may unsubscribe from email updates using an unsubscribe option when provided.
  • You may request access, correction, or deletion of information you previously submitted, subject to applicable requirements.
  • You may clear local storage through your browser settings to remove saved display preferences.

Children’s privacy

Free HTL Guide is intended for adult learners and laboratory professionals. The site is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Educational disclaimer

Free HTL Guide is an independent educational resource. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by ASCP or the ASCP Board of Certification. Site content does not replace an employer’s procedures, regulatory requirements, laboratory policies, professional judgment, or official examination guidance.

Policy updates

This policy may be updated as the site adds new services or changes how information is handled. The revised date at the top of this page will show when the latest version became effective.

Contact

Privacy questions or data requests may be sent to withnati@gmail.com.
