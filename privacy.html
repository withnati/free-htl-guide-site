Privacy and data choices

Privacy Policy

This policy explains what Free HTL Guide collects, why it is used, which service providers are involved, and how to control optional analytics.

Effective July 31, 2026

Key points

  • The lessons and downloads do not require an account.
  • Email signup is optional and requires consent.
  • Personal information is not sold.
  • Analytics is currently disabled.
  • Any future analytics activation requires explicit consent.

1. Site operator

Free HTL Guide is an independent educational website operated by Natnale Mengesha. Privacy questions and data requests may be sent to withnati@gmail.com.

2. Information you choose to provide

The email update form collects:

  • The email address you enter.
  • Your affirmative consent to receive occasional Free HTL Guide resource and module updates.
  • Basic form metadata such as the source page and subscription type.

The form does not ask for your name, employer, certification status, quiz answers, study notes, or other profile information.

3. Formspree processing

Email signup submissions are sent to Formspree, which processes and stores the submission so the request can be reviewed and, when configured, can send a confirmation or autoresponse. Formspree handles that information under its own terms and privacy practices.

Do not submit sensitive personal, patient, employment, financial, or health information through the email form.

4. How email information is used

  • To confirm that a signup request was received.
  • To provide occasional notices about new learning modules, practice tools, downloads, or major site updates.
  • To respond to an unsubscribe, correction, access, or deletion request.
  • To prevent spam, duplicate submissions, or abuse of the form.

Email addresses are not sold or used for third-party advertising.

5. Unsubscribing and deletion

You may stop future update messages at any time through an unsubscribe link included in an email or through the unsubscribe request page. Until an automated mailing-list provider is connected, unsubscribe requests may be processed manually.

You may also request access, correction, or deletion of an email address previously submitted to the site by contacting withnati@gmail.com from the same address.

6. Optional analytics and explicit consent

Analytics is currently disabled. The repository contains an analytics-ready measurement system, but its controlled configuration does not contain an active Google Analytics 4 Measurement ID. The site therefore does not intentionally send visitor analytics to Google Analytics at this time.

If analytics is activated later, the Google tag will not load until a visitor gives explicit consent through the site’s Privacy choices control. Declining analytics will be remembered on that device and will not load the Google analytics tag. A visitor may reopen Privacy choices and change the decision later.

The approved event set is limited to page and feature usage such as page views, module openings, scroll milestones, file downloads, quiz starts and completion percentages, study-plan task toggles, sharing actions, and email signup success or failure. The system is designed not to send email addresses, personal names, personal notes, quiz answers, question-level responses, patient information, or user IDs.

Event URLs are reduced to their origin and path before transmission so query strings and fragments are not included. Google Signals and advertising-personalization settings are disabled in the site configuration. The intended GA4 event-data retention setting is 14 months.

7. Privacy choices and browser storage

The Privacy choices control is added to the site footer. When analytics remains disabled, it explains that no analytics requests are being sent. If analytics is activated, it provides equally available Allow analytics and Decline analytics choices.

The site uses browser local storage to remember features such as dark mode, checked study-plan tasks, saved personal notes, last-read sections, quiz scores, and an analytics consent choice. Study notes and quiz answers remain in your browser and are not submitted through the email form or analytics system. Clearing browser storage removes these items from that device.

Session storage may briefly record that an email signup succeeded so the confirmation page can be shown correctly.

8. Analytics testing and safeguards

A local analytics debug mode is available to the site operator for quality assurance. It displays sanitized events in the browser and does not by itself enable Google Analytics. Automated checks prevent a static Google tag, an enabled configuration without a valid Measurement ID, unreviewed event names, prohibited data fields, or a mismatch between the configuration and this policy.

Quiz measurement is limited to aggregate score information such as total score, percentage, score band, and whether the study target was met. Individual answer selections and question text are not included.

9. Hosting, downloads, and technical logs

The website and downloadable files are hosted through GitHub Pages. Hosting providers and network services may generate ordinary technical logs, such as IP address, request time, browser information, and requested URL, as part of operating and securing their services.

10. Retention and sharing

Information is retained only as long as reasonably needed to provide requested updates, maintain suppression or unsubscribe records, address abuse, or satisfy legal and operational requirements. Information may be shared with service providers needed to operate the website and email form, or when disclosure is legally required.

If optional GA4 analytics is activated, the property should use a 14-month event-data retention setting. Aggregate reports may remain available beyond the underlying user-level event retention period. Analytics data will not be sold or used for third-party advertising by Free HTL Guide.

11. Children and educational scope

The site is intended for adult learners and laboratory professionals and is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Free HTL Guide is not affiliated with or endorsed by ASCP or the ASCP Board of Certification. Educational content does not replace official examination guidance, validated laboratory procedures, safety requirements, regulatory obligations, or professional judgment.

12. Policy changes

This policy may be revised when the site activates analytics, adds a mailing-list platform, paid services, user accounts, or other data-handling features. The effective date at the top will be updated when material changes are made.
