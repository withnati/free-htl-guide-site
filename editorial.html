1. Independence and scope

Free HTL Guide is an independent educational resource created by Natnale Mengesha, HTL(ASCP)cm. It is not affiliated with, sponsored by, approved by, or endorsed by the American Society for Clinical Pathology or the ASCP Board of Certification.

The guide does not reproduce confidential examination questions. Its practice questions are original educational items designed to test broadly published histotechnology knowledge and problem-solving skills.

Not a substitute for official information: candidates should confirm eligibility, examination policies, content guidelines, and testing details directly with the ASCP BOC before applying or testing.

3. Source hierarchy

  1. Official examination resources: the current public ASCP BOC content guideline and suggested reading list establish the alignment framework.
  2. Established histotechnology texts: current or widely recognized references such as Carson and Cappellano, Bancroft and Gamble, Brown, and HTL-appropriate immunohistochemistry texts support technical explanations.
  3. Primary regulatory and safety sources: applicable government regulations and official safety information are preferred for hazard and compliance topics.
  4. Validated laboratory documents: local SOPs, instrument instructions, assay instructions for use, safety data sheets, and quality requirements govern actual laboratory work.

Sources are summarized and cited; copyrighted books, examination questions, proprietary SOPs, and restricted documents are not reproduced.

4. Practice-question standards

Each module contains ten original multiple-choice questions. Questions are reviewed against the following criteria:

  • One best answer is supported by the lesson and reference set.
  • Distractors are plausible but are not trick answers based on obscure wording.
  • The explanation states the principle or corrective reasoning, not only the answer letter.
  • Local-policy questions identify the need to follow validated SOPs rather than presenting one facility’s practice as universal.
  • Questions do not claim to predict actual examination items or scores.
Foundational
Recall or explain a core fact, sequence, reagent, structure, or principle.
Application
Apply a principle to select a method, control, reagent, or next step.
Troubleshooting
Interpret a failure pattern and choose the most appropriate corrective action.
Target score
80% is a study target, not an examination-pass prediction.

5. General principles versus SOP-dependent practice

Histotechnology methods vary by specimen, laboratory, instrument, reagent system, validation, intended use, and regulation. The guide separates durable principles from operational details that require local control.

Durable principles: reagent function, reaction chemistry, artifact patterns, control logic, preservation concepts, and quality-system reasoning.
SOP-dependent details: exact concentrations, times, temperatures, instrument programs, control acceptance criteria, retest rules, exposure controls, waste procedures, and escalation pathways.

6. Review dates and version numbers

Each module displays a semantic version and an exact editorial review date. Version changes follow this convention:

  • Patch change — wording, formatting, citation, or minor clarification without changing the learning conclusion.
  • Minor change — new explanation, question revision, reference update, or meaningful content expansion.
  • Major change — substantial restructuring or a change that alters important conclusions or study strategy.

Content is reviewed when the public examination guideline changes, when a credible correction is received, or when a source update materially affects the lesson. A displayed date means editorial review, not independent peer review by ASCP or another certifying body.

7. Corrections and change log

Correction reports should identify the exact page, passage or question, the concern, and a supporting source when available. Do not send patient information, case identifiers, employer-confidential material, proprietary SOPs, or examination content.
