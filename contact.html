What this contact channel can address

Appropriate

  • Site corrections and source questions.
  • Broken links or download problems.
  • Suggestions for modules, quizzes, or printable tools.
  • General educational feedback.
  • Potential educational collaboration.

Outside the site’s role

  • Individual medical or diagnostic advice.
  • Official certification eligibility determinations.
  • Replacement of laboratory supervision or validated procedures.
  • Review of confidential patient or employer information.
  • Guaranteed examination predictions.

Messages are reviewed as availability allows. The page does not promise a specific response time.
