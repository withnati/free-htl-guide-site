Questions and answers

Frequently asked questions

Learn how to use the site, what the current lessons cover, and how the material fits into HT and HTL preparation.

What is Free HTL Guide?

Free HTL Guide is an independent educational website that organizes histology concepts into structured lessons, review tools, and printable resources for people preparing for HT or HTL certification and for professionals strengthening their bench knowledge.

Is this an official ASCP website?

No. Free HTL Guide is not affiliated with or endorsed by ASCP or the ASCP Board of Certification. The site is an independent study resource and should be used alongside the current official exam content outline, eligibility information, and candidate guidance.

What is the difference between HT and HTL?

Both certifications require a strong understanding of core histotechnology. HT preparation emphasizes essential tissue preparation, staining, safety, and laboratory knowledge. HTL preparation includes those foundations and generally requires greater depth in troubleshooting, quality systems, validation, laboratory operations, and molecular-adjacent methods.

Which modules are currently available?

The current site includes lessons covering fixation, processing and decalcification, and embedding and microtomy. Additional areas such as routine staining, special stains, laboratory operations, safety, IHC/ISH, and broader exam review are planned for future development.

Do I need laboratory access?

No. You can study the written lessons, review the examples, and use the downloadable resources without laboratory access. Bench practice can reinforce the material when it is available and appropriately supervised, but it is not required to begin learning through the site.

How long should I spend on each module?

A first pass through a module may take about 30–60 minutes, depending on your experience and how deeply you review the examples. Difficult topics should be revisited in shorter sessions, followed by practice questions or recall exercises rather than one long reading session.

Are the downloads really free?

Yes. The current starter resources are offered as free study and bench-reference tools. Some resources can be downloaded directly, while the email form may also be used to request materials and receive future site updates.

Will the site include quizzes and mock exams?

Interactive quizzes, answer explanations, and broader practice tools are part of the planned expansion. Until those features are complete, the existing modules should be used for concept review and structured note-taking rather than as a complete exam simulation.

Can I use the material as a laboratory procedure?

No. The content is educational and does not replace your laboratory’s validated procedures, safety requirements, manufacturer instructions, quality system, regulatory obligations, or supervisory guidance. Always follow the approved procedure used by your institution.

How can I report an error or suggest a topic?

Send the page name, the specific statement or issue, and any supporting detail to withnati@gmail.com. Clear correction reports and topic suggestions help improve future updates.
