About Free HTL Guide

Histology education built for both the exam and the bench

Free HTL Guide turns large, technical histology topics into structured lessons, practical explanations, focused review tools, and printable resources.

Explore the modules Read the FAQ

What guides the site

Exam-aligned

Content is organized around the knowledge areas HT and HTL candidates are expected to understand.

Bench-practical

Concepts are connected to tissue quality, artifacts, workflow decisions, and troubleshooting.

Clear and accessible

Lessons are written to make difficult material easier to review without stripping away important detail.

Mission

Make quality HT/HTL preparation easier to access

Histology candidates often have to piece together study material from textbooks, workplace notes, isolated practice questions, and scattered online references. Free HTL Guide was created to bring those pieces into one organized learning path.

The goal is not simply to memorize facts. The goal is to understand why a process works, recognize what went wrong when it does not, and connect exam concepts to real laboratory practice.

Free HTL Guide is an independent educational resource and is not affiliated with or endorsed by ASCP or the ASCP Board of Certification.

Creator and instructor

Natnale Mengesha

HTL(ASCP)cm Molecular pathology Boston University graduate training

Natnale is a working histology and molecular pathology professional who created Free HTL Guide to make technical exam material more organized, practical, and approachable. The teaching style emphasizes clear mechanisms, real workflow consequences, artifact troubleshooting, and the details that help learners reason through unfamiliar questions.

The long-term vision is to expand the site into a broader learning platform with additional lessons, practice questions, staining resources, and bench-ready tools while preserving a strong free educational foundation.
